Meet Ann Marie

Experience in the Madison market.

Ann Marie Battaglia brings experience in business, finance and sales to helping clients with one of their most important investments. She works with buyers and sellers in Morris, Union, Somerset and Essex Counties.

She graduated magna cum laude from Virginia Tech with concentrations in finance, business law and insurance. Before real estate, she was Assistant Treasurer and National Account Representative for Cash Management Sales at First Fidelity Bank (Wachovia), and a Corporate Lending Officer to Austrian subsidiaries in the US for European American Bank.

A Madison resident for more than 30 years, Ann Marie has served on local booster club boards, as treasurer of the Madison Junior School PTO, and as a fundraiser for local schools and athletic programs. She raises funds for ovarian cancer through Kaleidoscope of Hope, supports Turn the Towns Teal, and is a longtime cycling instructor at the Madison YMCA.

"My priority is always keeping a client/realtor relationship based on trust and integrity. I am dedicated to helping my clients throughout their journey in finding or selling their home. As their advocate, my clients have a hand held experience where I guide them seamlessly through their buying and selling process."Ann Marie Battaglia

Professional memberships: National Association of Realtors, New Jersey Board of Realtors, Garden State Multiple Listing Service.

The Rose City

About Madison.

  1. 1856Rose growing begins in Madison's greenhouses. Its roses later ship daily by train to New York, earning the name Rose City.
  2. 1867Drew University is founded in Madison.
  3. 1889Madison incorporates as a borough.
  4. 1996Midtown Direct service begins, linking Madison station to New York Penn Station.
  5. TodayShops and restaurants line Main Street and Waverly Place, near the train station.
